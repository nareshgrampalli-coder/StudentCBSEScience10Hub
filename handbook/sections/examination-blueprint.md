## Examination overview

**Applicable course:** CBSE Class X Science (086), academic year 2026-27; board examination 2027.

The official CBSE curriculum is the authority for prescribed topics, practical work, and assessment. The curriculum document is linked below. Its subject PDF could not be reliably text-extracted during this handbook's source review, so this page deliberately does not state unit marks, chapter weightings, question counts, or an official practical list.

Prepare to explain scientific ideas, interpret observations and data, use models and equations with correct units, and justify conclusions with evidence. These are general study recommendations, not an official description of the examination blueprint.

**Primary source:** [CBSE Academic Curriculum 2026-27](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/curriculum_2027.html) | [Science syllabus PDF](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/SecPart1/ScienceSt_SecP1_2026-27.pdf)
