## Final revision sheet

### Physics quantities and equations

- Current: `I = Q/t`; potential difference: `V = W/Q`; Ohm's law: `V = IR` when the stated conditions hold.
- Electrical power: `P = VI = I^2R = V^2/R`; heating: `H = I^2Rt` for constant current and resistance.
- Series resistors: `Rtotal = R1 + R2 + ...`; parallel resistors: `1/Rtotal = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + ...`.
- For mirrors and lenses, use the sign convention taught in class. The conventions shown in Chapter 9 must be applied consistently.

### Chemistry and biology reminders

- Balance equations by changing coefficients, not formula subscripts; check each element on both sides.
- Distinguish observation from inference, and identify conditions that can affect a test.
- Keep breathing distinct from cellular respiration; excretion distinct from egestion; arteries/veins by direction relative to the heart.
- Treat inheritance ratios as model probabilities, not guaranteed outcomes for a small number of offspring.

### Environment reminders

- Food-chain arrows point from food to consumer. Matter cycles; energy flows and becomes less available at successive trophic levels.
- Support claims about environmental causes with observations and evidence; distinguish correlation from demonstrated cause.

This is an original revision aid, not an official formula list. Verify prescribed formulae, conventions, and syllabus boundaries against the current CBSE curriculum and textbook.
