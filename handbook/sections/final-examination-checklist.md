## Final examination checklist

Before submitting a response, check:

- Have I answered the exact question and included a reason where one is requested?
- Are equations balanced, formulae unchanged, symbols defined, and chemical names used precisely?
- Are circuit calculations shown with SI units and a suitable equation?
- Have I stated the optical sign convention and checked image interpretation against a ray diagram?
- Are biological processes distinguished correctly and diagrams labelled clearly?
- Do data-based conclusions follow from the evidence, with limitations or controls noted where relevant?
- Have I checked the current official syllabus and school instructions rather than relying on assumed chapter weightings?

Use this as a self-check, not an official examination rubric.
