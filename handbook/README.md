## Scope and source notes

- **Board and subject:** Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), Class X Science, subject code 086.
- **Session:** Academic year 2026–27; board examination 2027.
- **Primary curriculum reference:** [CBSE Academic — Curriculum 2026–27](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/curriculum_2027.html); [Science syllabus PDF](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/SecPart1/ScienceSt_SecP1_2026-27.pdf), listed under Secondary Curriculum, Part 1.
- **Textbook reference:** Use the current NCERT Class X Science textbook available through [NCERT](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php).
- **Verification limitation:** The official curriculum page and subject PDF were located, but the PDF contents could not be reliably text-extracted during preparation. Therefore this handbook does not assert unit marks, chapter weightings, question counts, or an official practical list. Confirm the prescribed topics and assessment details against the official document and school instructions before the examination.
- **Review date:** 8 November 2026.

## How to use

Read the explanation and vocabulary first, then attempt the questions without looking at the feedback. Check units, conditions, and reasoning in every answer. For investigations, follow the teacher's instructions and school laboratory safety rules; do not perform chemical or electrical experiments without supervision.

## Originality and review

All explanations, worked examples, and practice questions in these pages are written for this handbook. No past-paper provenance is claimed. The material has not undergone independent review by a qualified Science educator; unresolved syllabus alignment should be checked with the official curriculum and teacher.
